# 001: Our universe is inside a black hole

**Status:** open

## The idea, in the human's words

> "Our universe is inside a black hole", or as I understand it: the edges of our universe is very similar to the event horizon of a black hole, and basically a black hole turned inside out would practically resemble a 3D universe as we know it, with the size and age of the black hole somewhat determining the size and age of that universe inside.

More depth to come from the human; this card will grow with it.

## Claude's reading

Draft, not yet confirmed. The statement bundles at least three separate ideas, which can succeed or fail independently:

1. **The edge idea.** The boundary of what we can see (or can ever reach) behaves like a black hole's event horizon.
2. **The inside-out idea.** The geometry inside a black hole, suitably flipped, is the geometry of an expanding universe like ours.
3. **The inheritance idea.** The parent black hole's mass and age set our universe's size and age.
