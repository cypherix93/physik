# 001: Our universe is inside a black hole

**Status:** wounded
**Started:** 2026-11-17

## The idea, in the human's words

First statement (2026-11-17):

> "Our universe is inside a black hole", or as I understand it: the edges of our universe is very similar to the event horizon of a black hole, and basically a black hole turned inside out would practically resemble a 3D universe as we know it, with the size and age of the black hole somewhat determining the size and age of that universe inside.

The fuller picture (2026-11-17):

> if you imagine a 2D universe, a black hole can be expressed an infinite cone-shaped hole in the 3rd dimension, with the bottom of the hole being the singularity. but the sides of that cone-shaped hole would still remain that 2D universe. so if you flip that structure in time, the creation of that 2D universe inside the black hole would seem to come from the singularity (bottom of the hole), and the future would be towards the mouth of the black hole. and if the black hole keeps growing bigger (as in consumes more information), it would keep creating "new future" for the 2D universe inside of it. and since only the sides of a black hole is required for a 2D universe to survive to be expressed, each 2D universe-shaped cone can have their own black holes.
>
> the other interesting thing that's tickling my brain is that mass of a black hole is almost entirely proportional to its surface area - which gives weight to the surface of that cone-shaped hole being the entirety of that 2D universe.
>
> another thing this sorta aligns with is the acceleration expansion of our own universe, which is exactly what you get when you start getting closer to the mouth of that cone-shaped hole (obv the cone isn't uniform, it's more like tapered, kind of like a cyclone)
>
> so essentially, you'd take that 2D shape and apply to our 3D space almost exactly.

A sketch from the human is expected; it goes in `sketches/`.

## Claude's reading

Draft, not yet confirmed. The funnel is a history of the universe, not a place. Depth is time: the singularity at the bottom is the beginning, the mouth is the future, and each horizontal slice through the funnel is the whole of space at one moment. The width of the funnel at a given depth is the size of the universe at that time, so the funnel's outline is the expansion history. The universe is the funnel's wall, with nothing inside it.

## What would kill it

- **Already landed:** the black hole's shape alone does not cause the acceleration (experiment 001). The theory survives only if something ties the expansion inside to the horizon.
- If no rule linking the horizon to the expansion can match the measured expansion history, C3 is dead in every form.
- If the time flip cannot be reconciled with a black hole that keeps feeding, C4 is dead.
- More to be added as claims are tested.

## Open questions

- Is the universe the wall of the funnel (same dimensions as the parent) or the information written on the horizon (one dimension fewer, the holographic idea)? These are different theories.
- The time flip turns a black hole that eats into one that spits. How does C4 survive that?
- Answered by experiment 001: putting matter inside fixes the unevenness but not the lack of acceleration.
- What could carry information from the horizon to the inside? Candidates to look up: a horizon term acting as dark energy (Gaztañaga), and holographic dark energy, where the horizon's size sets the dark energy density. Both from memory, unverified.
